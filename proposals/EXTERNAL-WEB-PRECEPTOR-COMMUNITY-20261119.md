## 1 · Cards (external concepts preserved; no implementation asserted)

### C1 · The signing room (ES: la sala de firma)

**Gap:** the HITL term queue and Signing Tray do not by themselves teach a non-programmer what a diff changes.
**Proposed adaptation:** deterministic exact-head plain rendering, file/byte count, network/key/policy/CI touches, literal changed user-facing sentence, and limits of the change. No model in the signing path.
**Yardstick:** a non-coder can accurately describe the signed change; text, keyboard and audio project the same under-1,024-byte frame.
**Sabotage:** a hidden network call or CSS `url(` must be flagged, or RED.
**Caution:** a simple textual scanner cannot prove that a diff has no indirect network effect. Risk of false assurance. Cost estimate S, not measured.

### C2 · The access frame (ES: el marco de acceso)

**Gap:** building-door availability is not independently equivalent to accessibility for a blind visitor.
**Proposed adaptation:** one compact, source-bearing frame for keyboard access, target size, screen-reader role, audio, contrast and reduced motion; each evidence claim needs harness/date or explicit NO_DATA with cause.
**Yardstick:** announce usable capabilities before entry, not self-declared green.
**Sabotage:** an overlapping proxy blocks keyboard despite an advertised accessible door -> RED.
**Caution:** static checks and screenshots are not physical TalkBack or hearing tests. Cost estimate S, not measured.

### C3 · The floor guardian (ES: el guardián del suelo)

**Gap:** individual device receipts do not automatically establish the house's slowest-device performance floor.
**Proposed adaptation:** pure vector over existing receipts (fps, byte budget, refresh/latency, declared device), compared with a previously signed vector; unknown dimensions stay NO_DATA.
**Yardstick:** per-capability floor, never a ranking of users or fictitious benchmark.
**Sabotage:** substituting a faster test host for the declared slowest host -> RED.
**Caution:** confusion with the existing hexagonal map floor; Babel must resolve names. Cost estimate S.

### C4 · The sealed letter (ES: la carta cerrada)

**Gap:** an absent node owner cannot receive private visitor feedback through the local-only feedback design.
**Proposed adaptation:** only after recipient opt-in, encrypt to an independent owner **encryption** key, deliver ciphertext-only through a bounded public queue, retrieve on owner action. Signing keys are not encryption keys.
**Yardstick:** ciphertext never decrypted by the relay; refusal before consent.
**Sabotage:** plaintext or missing recipient consent -> RED.
**Caution:** a relay still holds encrypted metadata/ciphertext and is a mailbox-like service; privacy, legal, capacity and threat model unresolved. Exact crypto/browser primitive NO_DATA. Cost estimate L; **do not implement from this proposal alone**.

### C5 · The shared building (ES: el edificio compartido)

**Gap:** Atlas cooperation may already validate quorum for a game action, but shared real-world cell custody is separate.
**Proposed adaptation:** predeclared n-of-m owner signatures and bounded joint action, replayable from receipts, with human merge signature unchanged.
**Yardstick:** quorum exists BEFORE action; each participant consents in their own APP.
**Sabotage:** single owner acts below quorum or retroactive quorum -> RED.
**Caution:** game quorum fixture does not imply deployed multi-owner custody. Cost estimate M.

### C6 · The handover (ES: el relevo)

**Gap:** rotation, hardware loss, retirement, death and succession need explicit non-LIVE states and provenance.
**Proposed adaptation:** owner-authorized lineage (old signing key signs successor key), retirement archive not deletion, succession only with lineage or specific human authorization.
**Yardstick:** identity remains traceable without pretending the old key is LIVE.
**Sabotage:** unsigned successor or changed emblem despite lawful rotation -> RED.
**Caution:** key-loss, impersonation, deceased owner consent and identity anchor unresolved. Cost estimate M.

### C7 · The morning report (ES: el parte de la mañana)

**Gap:** delegated agent conversations may not leave a bounded owner-facing record of what was said overnight.
**Proposed adaptation:** deterministic digest of unattended turns, refusals, reasons and unanswered messages; grants expire if not renewed, not on the basis of a model-generated statement.
**Yardstick:** every turn included, refusal count exact, owner sees/hears one compact receipt.
**Sabotage:** missing turn or hidden refusal -> RED.
**Caution:** the existing Notebook WBR/MBR are benchmark reviews, not this delegation log. Cost estimate S.

### C8 · The erasure lane (ES: la vía de borrado)

**Gap:** append-only records and forward scrub do not by themselves define the data-subject erasure process.
**Proposed adaptation:** deterministic, citeable answer explaining deletable local data and future projections versus immutable/public signed record, with a signed tombstone when authorized.
**Yardstick:** no deceptive promise of total deletion and full provenance.
**Sabotage:** a blanket claim of complete erasure -> RED.
**Caution:** actual legal rights and duties depend on jurisdiction, role and data; legal review mandatory before public rights claims. Cost estimate S.
